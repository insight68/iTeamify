aurocreator uses https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send so users can send customer emails from the workspace assistant using their connected Gmail account. Users prepare a message from customer context, review the sender, recipients, subject and body, and explicitly confirm sending. Only then does the backend call Gmail users.messages.send with the approved message. This permission is used to send the approved email; it does not grant inbox-reading or mailbox-management access. openid, userinfo.email and userinfo.profile identify users but cannot send mail. gmail.readonly and gmail.metadata also cannot send mail. Gmail add-on-only scopes are limited to Gmail add-on interactions and do not support our standalone web app. gmail.send is therefore the narrowest scope for this sending function. gmail.compose, gmail.modify and full mailbox access would grant capabilities this function does not require.
