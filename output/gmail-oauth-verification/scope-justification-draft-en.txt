aurocreator requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send for customer email communication from its workspace assistant. We are implementing a flow in which users connect their Gmail account, prepare a message from customer context, review the sender, recipients, subject and body, and explicitly confirm sending. The backend will call Gmail users.messages.send with the approved message. This permission will be used to send the approved email, not to read or manage the mailbox. openid, userinfo.email and userinfo.profile identify users but cannot send mail. gmail.readonly and gmail.metadata also cannot send mail. Gmail add-on-only scopes do not support this standalone web app. gmail.send is the narrowest scope for this sending function; gmail.compose, gmail.modify and full mailbox access are unnecessary. The sending function is not yet available in the current build; we will submit verification after it is implemented and demonstrated.
